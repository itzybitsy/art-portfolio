Bio

I am a neurodivergent first-generation immigrant from the Mexico-US border. Being from the border has made me navigate two different worlds and live in the in-between. I have a background in fine art and printmaking. Recently I exhibited my work at Bronx Art Space for the exhibition Te Amo Porque S.O.S Pueblo. It received positive reviews from the New York Times.

Artist Statement

My work is influenced by art from central Mexico. I explore the visual languages of my ancestors the "Mexikah" and their relationship to the land. I am also inspired by the "Nopales" prickly pear cactus from the Chihuahuan desert, where I am from. The prickly is very important to my identity I grew up surrounded by cactuses and watch them change to different shades around the year. I was always mesmerized to watch them flowering with very little water. They are part of my culture, we eat them, and decorate our homes. The city of Tenochtitlan, modern-day Mexico City, was founded when the Mexicas found an eagle standing on a cactus. This is why it's our national emblem. I feel the prickly represents the resiliency of Mexicans to adapt and overcome hardship.
